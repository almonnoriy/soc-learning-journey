# Introduction to Cybersecurity
## What is Cybersecurity?
Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, networks, mobile devices, servers, and data from digital attacks, theft, damage, or unauthorized access.
In simple terms, it’s like putting strong locks, security cameras, and guards on your digital house. Just as we protect our physical belongings in the real world, we also need to protect our digital life — bank accounts, personal photos, work documents, emails, and even critical systems like hospitals and power grids.
## Why Cybersecurity Matters Today
We live in a world where almost everything is connected to the internet. From online banking to smart homes, from government services to hospital records — if it’s digital, it can be targeted.
Here’s why it’s so important:
- Hackers can steal money directly from people’s accounts.
- Companies can lose millions (sometimes billions) of dollars from a single breach.
- Personal information like photos, chats, ID cards, and medical records can be leaked and misused.
- Attacks on hospitals or electricity systems can put real human lives at risk.
- As more people work remotely and use cloud services, the number of possible targets keeps growing.
Cyber attacks are no longer just a “tech problem” — they affect everyone.
## The Main Goals of Cybersecurity (CIA)
Security professionals use a simple but powerful model called the **CIA Triad** to guide their work:
- **Confidentiality**: Keeping information private. Only the right people should be able to see it.
- **Integrity**: Making sure data is accurate and hasn’t been secretly changed or tampered with.
- **Availability**: Ensuring systems and data are available and working when we need them.
If any of these three is compromised, a security incident has occurred.
## Different Roles in Cybersecurity
Cybersecurity is a big field with many career paths:
- **Blue Team (Defenders)**: We focus on protecting systems, monitoring for attacks, and responding when something goes wrong. SOC Analysts and Incident Responders belong here.
- **Red Team (Attackers)**: Ethical hackers who try to break into systems to find weaknesses before real attackers do.
- **Purple Team**: A mix of both — they work together to improve defenses.
- **Leaders & Architects**: They design security strategies, policies, and manage teams.
